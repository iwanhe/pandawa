### **B. Budget Efficiency Ratio**

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
BUDGET EFFICIENCY CALCULATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Budget Efficiency % = (Planned Budget - Actual Spend) 
                     ────────────────────────────── × 100%
                         Planned Budget

Example:
  Planned Budget: Rp.  1,000,000,000
  Actual Spend:    Rp.   850,000,000
  Variance:       Rp.  150,000,000
  
  Efficiency = (1,000 - 850) / 1,000 × 100% = 15% UNDER BUDGET

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
```
